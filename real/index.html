WHAT THE HOOKS ARE (honest)

  • x̂0 lens — at every step the DDIM estimate x̂0 = (x_t − √(1−ᾱ)ε̂)/√ᾱ is decoded with TAESD (1.2M-param tiny autoencoder). This is the diffusion analogue of the logit lens.
  • Token attribution — causal, not attention: each prompt token's text-encoder output is replaced by the padding-position embedding and the change in step-1 x̂0 is measured (scalar + 64×64 spatial map). The ONNX graphs do not expose cross-attention probabilities, so we do not draw attention maps.
  • Prompt swap — conditioning patching: prompt A for the first k steps, B afterwards (prompt-to-prompt-style, without attention injection).
  • Steering — adds α·(mean E(+) − mean E(−)) to every text-encoder position.
  • CFG sweep — same seed, guidance 1 / 3 / 7.5 / 12.
